# User guide

DaliCandy is one plug-in. Put it on an instrument track and play / sequence it with MIDI; put it on an audio track (or enable its audio input) and it processes and analyses that audio while adding candy on top. Dry level, sidechain analysis source, capture and live negative space only have an effect while an audio input is connected.

The plug-in opens on the **Kitchen**, the simple main screen. The **ADVANCED** button (top right) reveals the five full pages (Generate, Sequence, Synthesis, Modulation, Track analysis) described below. The header holds the preset browser (prev / next / menu / search / save / favourite / random), the page tabs and the output meter. The footer has Out gain, Candy level (and Dry level in the FX), Quality (Eco / Standard / High), status, CPU, voices and the output-protection counter.

## 0 Kitchen (main screen)
**Six families, every pair a fusion.** SNAP (clicks, blips, chirps), CHIME (bells, metal ticks, springs), TWITCH (stutters, slices, bit shreds), SWIRL (sweeps, spirals, ring-mod oddities), MOSS (breaths, insects, wet clusters) and HAZE (washes, clouds, risers). The 6x6 **flavour matrix** has the 6 pure families on the diagonal and the 15 pairs off it; every pair has its own dish name (e.g. CHIME x MOSS = Wind Chimes). The **row leads, the column blends in**, so CHIME-leads-MOSS and MOSS-leads-CHIME are two different dishes. Clicking a cell cooks eight new plates at once. A fusion is built from layers of both families (up to 4 layers); its modulation is re-routed so neither half loses its movement.
**ROLL** ignores your choice: random family or fusion, random seasoning, and one of the eight plates is picked for you.
**COOK** makes eight new plates from the chosen family / fusion. With **KEEP** on, COOK makes eight variations of the *current* dish instead: same skeleton (engines, layer count, envelopes, levels, sends, inserts), different details (parameters, pitch, seeds, modulation).
**SPICE** adds weirdness and motion (deeper and faster modulation, chaotic routes, odd inserts such as frequency shift / ring mod / wavefolder / comb). **DRIFT** makes repeats evolve: each hit wanders a little further in pitch, timbre and length from the previous one (a bounded random walk, deterministic). DRIFT 0 = repeats stay the same. BRIGHT and MOTION steer the generator.
**LENGTH** is Free or a tempo-synced window from 1/16 to 4 bars (4/4) at the host tempo (the Analysis BPM setting is used when the host gives no tempo). The generator fits envelopes and tails until the audible length lands in 50-130 % of the window and rejects what it cannot fit; the dragged / exported WAV is cut or padded to **exactly** the window (8 ms fade when cut) so it sits on the grid.
**LISTEN** captures the track (needs host transport and the audio input / sidechain, see *Analysis Source*), analyses it, places events in its gaps and aims the next generation at the quietest band between 400 Hz and 12 kHz. LISTEN stays active (steering) until you switch the *Listen* parameter off.
**Drag** the waveform or the big *DRAG THE DISH* pad into the DAW for a WAV; *DRAG PATTERN (MIDI)* drags the sequence as a MIDI file. The on-screen keyboard (C3-B4, `<` `>` change octave) plays the dish at any pitch; from a MIDI keyboard the note number sets the pitch (middle C = original pitch) and the MIDI channel 1-8 picks the sound slot.

## 1 Generate
Seven macros (defaults Complexity 65, Motion 75, Space 45, Density 38, Chaos 40, Brightness 50, Musicality 65), Category (8), Length (Short / Medium / Long / Custom ms), Root / Scale, Negative-space sensitivity. A family chosen in the Kitchen overrides *Category*; *Use category* switches back. **GENERATE** runs on a worker thread and returns 8 diverse candidates (3x oversampled, rejected for silence / clipping / DC / wrong length, scored, then picked for diversity; the taste model biases the choice). Click a card to make it current; *Use*, *Breed* (current x card), *Slot* (store in one of 8 sound slots = MIDI channel in the Instrument / per-event sound in the sequencer), *>* audition. Drag a waveform out to the DAW or file manager to drop a real WAV.
Shape the sound: **Mutate / Evolve** (amount knob), **Recall** (steps back through history), Audition, Like / Favourite / Dislike (teaches the local taste model), *New Ln* regenerates one layer, *Lock Ln* protects a layer from Mutate / Evolve / Regenerate. Export row: one-shot, loop, multi-bar, per-layer, dry/wet/FX-only, all candidates, MIDI; 44.1/48/88.2/96 kHz, 16 / 24 / 32f; "DRAG WAV" drags the current sound.

## 2 Sequence (fill the gaps of your track)
The sequencer is **off by default**: a fresh plug-in is silent on Play. The eight plates are the palette. Flow: put the track in (let the track play through the plug-in as an effect and press LISTEN, or - on an instrument track, where no audio arrives - press LISTEN and choose a WAV of the track, or drop the WAV on the window; the INPUT label on the Kitchen shows whether audio reaches the plug-in) -> BUILD FOR THIS TRACK -> switch on PLAY SEQUENCE.
BUILD picks, for each spot where the track has room, the plate that fits: free frequency band, enough room until the next hit, risers/transitions only before section changes. AMOUNT = how many events, VARIETY = how widely the eight plates are used, SURPRISE = how much NEW VARIATION changes, SWING, HUMANISE. The pattern view shows the track's loudness and hits in grey and your plates as coloured bars (number = plate); click an event to lock it. Without an analysed track BUILD cannot place by context and the pattern is rhythm only (the status line says so). MORE CONTROLS opens the full toolbox (modes, division, Euclid, ratchet, evolve, freeze).

## 3 Synthesis
Layer tabs (4), active / mute / solo / lock, engine choice, 16 named engine parameters (right-click a knob: lock the parameter or randomise it), envelope, pitch, pan, level, per-layer insert effect + sends, and the shared FX bus (delay, reverb, chorus/flanger/phaser, width, master gain, low cut). Bottom: rendered preview and 8-band spectrum of the sound.

## 4 Modulation
32 routes (source, destination, bipolar depth, curve, slew). Modulator editor: LFOs (7 shapes incl. 16-point custom curve, tempo sync), MSEG (drag points, shift-drag bends a segment), S&H, chaos, random walk, 16-step modulator, follower. Four host-automatable macros feed the matrix.

## 5 Track analysis
Arm **Capture** (4 / 8 / 16 / 32 bars; needs the audio input or sidechain to be connected, i.e. effect use); recording starts at the next bar line while the host plays. **Import WAV** works in every mode (the only option on an instrument track). Result: waveform, bar grid, section estimates, per-step *opportunity map*, 1/16-grid placement suggestions with score breakdown. Click the map to add your own hit; accept / reject / lock suggestions; **Apply to sequence** turns accepted ones into events. The masking panel compares candy vs track in 8 bands (an estimate, not a guarantee). The live panel shows level, bands, brightness, onsets, stereo correlation; *Live negative space* lets the FX leave out non-frozen events when the incoming audio is busy; *Adaptive mix* scales the conservative low-cut / dynamic-EQ / width / trim adaptation.

## Presets
`.dalicandy` JSON, versioned (current v2; v1 prototype files migrate on load; files from a newer version are refused with a message). Save writes to the user bank; factory presets are read-only.
